A boutique consultancy for leaders and organisations, co-designed around your people and never off the shelf.

BHD Asia leverages language, cultural, generational and professional diversity, along with the unique experiences of the team's personal journeys, to deliver impactful, tailored solutions for clients around the world.

Chapter IV

Four values, one confident voice.

What BHD Asia stands for, and how it sounds when it says so.

01

Human, not resources

Deep respect and appreciation for who we are. Empathy, psychological safety and courage at the centre of every recommendation.

02

True win-win

Collaborative, transparent partnerships that transfer knowledge and set clients up for long-term autonomy and success.

03

Diversity

Passionate about diversity of thoughts, approaches and methodologies, the only way to deliver the very best results.

04

Integrity

Ethical behaviour and full transparency in all communications, decisions, relationships and work practices.

Chapter IX

Navy buttons, gold silk.

The small, repeatable parts that make a BHD Asia layout feel like BHD Asia.

The button

One format everywhere: 48 px high, radius 12, Plus Jakarta Sans 600 15 px. Primary is navy with white text; secondary is white with a #D8DEE9 border. Hover lifts 2 px, a light sweep crosses and the arrow slides 4 px; a press sinks, a click sends a soft ripple from the pointer; focus is a 2 px navy ring. On dark bands: white primary, white outline secondary.

The two-bar accent

A thin red bar then a navy bar, echoing the logo. Once per layout, before an eyebrow or beside the URL. Never recoloured, never twice.

What do you need?

The eyebrow

Inter 700, 11-12 px caps, +.12em tracking, amber ink #8F5A00, above a section title.

The hero silk

HummingBeing’s fine gold hairlines drifting across the home hero on an 8 s breath. They flow around Isabelle and stay clear behind her photo: no disc, panel, glow or rays behind her.

101216pill

Radii

10 for small cards and event-card buttons, 12 for buttons, 16 for cards and panels, pill for chips, badges and the newsletter field.

Cards

White, radius 16, a 1 px #ECE5D9 hairline, no glow, no tilt, no coloured side stripe. Title and description always differ clearly in size (16 px over 13.5-14.5 px).

Services

The navigation underline

Navigation in navy with a 2 px marigold underline, offset 6 px, on the current page.

Chapter X

Real people, real rooms.

Isabelle’s own photographs lead; workshop scenes show the work happening. Light, bright, daylight. Never AI or stock images, never a mirrored photo of a real person.

AttendeesBlur only the eyes of workshop attendees; Isabelle, Simba and the team stay clear
BrightnessLift photos so whites read white; never dark or moody
CropFrame on the person; portrait sources need a full-width crop
SlidesNever show a projected slide that reads TRE®
Chapter XI

Calm pages, a living hero.

Sections are calm: no glows, tilt or auto-play. The home hero is the one place with rich motion. Every animation respects reduced motion.

Chapter XVI

Do this. Never that.

The short list to check a piece against before it goes out.

Do

  • Make posts, posters, thumbnails and ads in the HummingBeing format, with the BHD Asia mark in the lock-up.
  • Pastel light surfaces; navy is the action colour, marigold the soft accent.
  • Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and buttons, Inter for text.
  • Amber ink #8F5A00 for eyebrows, icons and orange text.
  • Real photos; blur only attendees’ eyes.
  • HummingBeing silk around Isabelle in the hero, nothing behind her.
  • The two-bar accent once per layout; TRE™ in every visible line.

Don't

  • Marigold as text, or as a button fill on light.
  • Shapes, discs, glows or rays behind Isabelle’s photo.
  • Highlighter marks on words.
  • Glows, tilt or spotlights on cards and sections.
  • The full-colour mark straight on navy.
  • AI or stock images; mirrored photos of real people.
  • Dark-mode versions of pages or posts.

House rules, every material

TRE™, alwaysTRE carries the TM symbol in all visible text. Never the registered mark, never bare. File names, URLs and form values are exempt.
One public emailbonjour@bhdasia.com. Never a personal address. Write it as plain text, not as a link.
No discount codesDiscount codes are never printed on posters, cards, captions or pages.
When Simba appearsUse his Living with the Spirit logo on that material.
The rainbow hummingbirdIt is Saymara's logo and belongs only on Saymara events.
The ICF CCE badgeOnly on ICF-accredited events.
Three brands, three rolesBHD Asia is the company. HummingBeing is Isabelle's coaching and somatic brand. TRE™ in Singapore is the community hub.
Check before quotingDates and prices change. Check the live events page first.
