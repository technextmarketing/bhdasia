A boutique consultancy for leaders and organisations, co-designed around your people and never off the shelf.

BHD Asia leverages language, cultural, generational and professional diversity, along with the unique experiences of the team's personal journeys, to deliver impactful, tailored solutions for clients around the world.

Chapter IV

Four values, one confident voice.

What BHD Asia stands for, and how it sounds when it says so.

01

Human, not resources

Deep respect and appreciation for who we are. Empathy, psychological safety and courage at the centre of every recommendation.

02

True win-win

Collaborative, transparent partnerships that transfer knowledge and set clients up for long-term autonomy and success.

03

Diversity

Passionate about diversity of thoughts, approaches and methodologies, the only way to deliver the very best results.

04

Integrity

Ethical behaviour and full transparency in all communications, decisions, relationships and work practices.

VoiceTalk about people and organisations, not products.

  • Confident
  • Warm
  • Specific
  • People, not products
  • Title Case headings

Write it like this

  • co-designed with your people
  • bespoke
  • never off-the-shelf
  • no obligation, no pitch decks

  • Book a Discovery Call

Avoid

  • revolutionary
  • game-changing

  • prices in event hero stats

Chapter IX

Pills, bars and warm light.

The small, repeatable parts that make a BHD Asia layout feel like BHD Asia.

The two-bar accent

A thin red bar then a navy bar, echoing the logo. Once per layout, as a mark before an eyebrow or beside the URL. Never recoloured, never twice.

The marigold pill

Marigold fill, ink label, Figtree 700 15 px, Title Case. Hover: a deeper marigold blooms from the pointer and the pill lifts 2 px. Secondary is translucent ivory with a faint ink border.

Leadership · Resilience · Growth

The eyebrow

Bold caps, +14% tracking, amber ink, led by the two bars. Used on posters and in this guide. The website hides eyebrows above headings.

Warm blobs

Warm blobs

Slow drifting sun and marigold washes over ivory behind hero areas. A very slow 20 s+ drift is the only looping motion.

Chapter XI

Soft, slow, never aggressive.

Gentle easing, 500 to 900 ms, with a little delight where natural. Every interaction respects reduced motion.

Ease soft

Hover and focus transitions.

cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) · 500 ms

Bloom

The button fill that grows from the pointer.

cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) · 800 ms

Reveal

Sections rise no more than 20 px.

cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) · 900 ms

The limits

Duration500 to 900 ms
Reveal travel20 px or less
Hover lift3 px or less
Tilt2 degrees or less
LoopsOnly a very slow drift of 20 s or more
Reduced motionRespected everywhere

Chapter XVI

Do this. Never that.

The short list to check a piece against before it goes out.

Do

  • Warm light surfaces; marigold is the action colour.
  • Ink text on marigold; amber ink #8F5A00 for orange text and the accent word.
  • Figtree everywhere; Title Case, never uppercase.
  • The two-bar accent once per layout.
  • The mark on a white tile over dark grounds or photos.
  • TRE™ in every visible line.

Don't

  • Marigold or sun as text on light.
  • The full-colour mark straight on navy.
  • Recolour, outline or shadow the bars.
  • Uppercase buttons.
  • Stock boardrooms.
  • Dark-mode versions of pages or posts.

House rules, every material

TRE™, alwaysTRE carries the TM symbol in all visible text. Never the registered mark, never bare. File names, URLs and form values are exempt.
One public emailbonjour@bhdasia.com. Never a personal address. Write it as plain text, not as a link.
No discount codesDiscount codes are never printed on posters, cards, captions or pages.
When Simba appearsUse his Living with the Spirit logo on that material.
The rainbow hummingbirdIt is Saymara's logo and belongs only on Saymara events.
The ICF CCE badgeOnly on ICF-accredited events.
Three brands, three rolesBHD Asia is the company. HummingBeing is Isabelle's coaching and somatic brand. TRE™ in Singapore is the community hub.
Check before quotingDates and prices change. Check the live events page first.
